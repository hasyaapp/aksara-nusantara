Aksara Nusantara

A Canva app that turns Latin text into Javanese script (aksara Jawa) and adds it to your design as editable text.

Help and support

How to type

  • é or è → taling (saté). Plain e → pepet (sega).
  • ng, ny, dh, th are single letters.
  • Numbers become Javanese numerals between pada pangkat.
  • Comma → pada lingsa, full stop → pada lungsi.
