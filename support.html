How to type

  • é or è → the e of saté. Plain e → pepet, the e of sega. Batak Toba and Rejang write both with one sign.
  • ng and ny are single letters.

Script notes

  • Javanese: dh and th are single letters. Numbers become Javanese numerals between pada pangkat. Comma → pada lingsa, full stop → pada lungsi.
  • Sundanese: type eu for paneuleung. Numbers appear between bars, as |123|.
  • Balinese: words are written joined, without spaces. Comma → carik siki, full stop → carik pareren.
  • Batak Toba: spelling of the 2022 Toba Cultural Congress: ka and ca use the Mandailing letters with tompi.
  • Lontara: final consonants and double letters are not written. Comma and full stop → pallawa.
  • Surat Ulu (Rejang): letters Rejang lacks use the nearest sound: f as p, v as b, z as j.
